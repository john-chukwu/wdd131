🌍 Explore Nigeria

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Explore Nigeria

The name was selected because the website focuses on introducing visitors to Nigeria's culture, tourism, beautiful destinations, food, history, and traditions. It is simple, memorable, and clearly describes the purpose of the website.

Optional Domain: explorenigeria.org

Site Purpose

Explore Nigeria provides visitors with useful information about Nigeria's tourist attractions, cultural heritage, traditional foods, major cities, festivals, and travel tips. The site helps local and international visitors discover interesting places to visit and learn more about the country's diversity.

Scenarios

  • What are the most beautiful tourist attractions in Nigeria?
  • Which Nigerian foods should first-time visitors try?
  • What cultural festivals take place during the year?
  • Which cities are best for tourism and relaxation?
